Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PeakCut handles information when you use the PeakCut desktop application and its related features.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, PeakCut may process the following information:

  • Scripts, text prompts, and other content you provide.
  • Images, videos, and audio files you import into the application.
  • Content generated or edited using PeakCut.
  • Basic TikTok profile information when you choose to connect your TikTok account.
  • Authorization tokens and publishing information needed to provide TikTok integration features.

2. How We Use Information

Information is processed to provide the application's features, create and edit videos, generate requested content, maintain authorized account connections, and allow users to upload or publish content through supported integrations.

3. Local Processing and AI Services

PeakCut is a desktop application. Files may be processed locally on your computer where supported by the selected features.

Some features may rely on external artificial intelligence services or other third-party providers. When you use a feature that requires an external provider, the relevant content or information may be transmitted to that provider for processing.

The providers used depend on the features and configuration available in your version of PeakCut. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

Before using external AI services, review their privacy policies and avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information unless you understand how it will be handled.

4. TikTok Integration

PeakCut may allow you to connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official authorization system.

When you choose to connect your account, PeakCut may receive basic profile information and authorization data permitted by the permissions you approve.

Depending on the permissions granted, PeakCut may use this access to upload videos as drafts or publish videos directly when you explicitly authorize the action.

PeakCut uses TikTok information only to provide the features you request. TikTok data is subject to TikTok's applicable terms and privacy policies.

You can revoke PeakCut's access through your TikTok account settings.

5. Sharing of Information

PeakCut does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with third-party providers only when necessary to provide a feature you request, with connected platforms when you authorize an action, or when disclosure is required by law.

Third-party providers handle information according to their own policies and applicable agreements.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Project files and imported content may be stored on your computer as part of your use of the desktop application.

Information processed by external providers or connected platforms may be retained according to their respective storage and retention policies.

Retention of authorization tokens and other integration data depends on the application's implementation and your account connection settings.

7. Data Security

We aim to protect information handled by PeakCut through reasonable technical and organizational measures. However, no software or electronic transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can choose which content to import and which features to use. You can also choose whether to connect your TikTok account and revoke that authorization through TikTok.

You may request information about personal data handled by PeakCut or ask questions about its deletion by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy

PeakCut is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as PeakCut evolves. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
